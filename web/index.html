Metric definitions & data notes

Commercial measures use delivered orders only. Order value = product prices + freight; it is not platform profit or net revenue. Customers use the unique-customer identifier. Repeat customers have at least two delivered orders within the current selection.

Delivery is late when its calendar date is after the estimated date. Missing delivery dates are excluded from on-time rates. Review averages use one prepared score per order; low reviews are scores of 1 or 2. Delay and review comparisons show association, not causation.

Click a bar or monthly point to filter the current page. Shared dropdown filters persist across pages; chart selections clear when you switch pages. Payment methods can overlap across orders. Category selection limits financial values to that category and order counts to matching orders.

The historical snapshot has sparse initial months and a partial final year. A small filtered sample can produce volatile rates. The web edition computes metrics from the same prepared snapshot; it is a browser implementation, not an embedded Power BI report.
